    \caption{Where each geometry's locked frequencies sit, in band $[2,250]$\,Hz at
    $f_s=512$\,Hz. Each branch is the harmonics of one spacing, so the spacing is the whole branch:
    the stride branch is the multiples of $f_s/S$, the patch branch the multiples of $f_s/P$, given
    once per group; \emph{in band} counts the stride branch's members. The two combs meet on a
    third, spaced $f_s/g$ with $g=\gcd(P,S)$, which is what the shared column counts; a spacing
    above the band means they never meet inside it and nothing is shared. \textbf{Stride-only} is
    what is left of the stride branch once the shared members are removed, and it is zero exactly
    when $S \mid P$: those are the only frequencies at which a loss is attributable to the stride
    alone. $|\mathcal F_{lock}|$ is the union of the two branches, not the sum.
