\subsection{Limits of this design}
The five runs were chosen for Deliverable~1 and they bound what can be concluded from the sweep above. Three of them have $P=S$, so their two branches coincide at every locked frequency and no observation in those geometries can be attributed to one branch. Stride-only sites exist in one geometry, $P{=}16,\,S{=}12$, and patch-only sites in two, so a claim about the stride branch rests on a single configuration and a claim about the patch branch on two. The configuration axes are equally short: the overlap ratio takes three values, and exactly one pair shares a stride while differing in patch size, which is the only place an effect of the patch can be separated from an effect of the stride. Every statement below is therefore conditional on a narrow design, and on one training seed per geometry.
