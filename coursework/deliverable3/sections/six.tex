Chronos-Bolt-Tiny is distributed with the standard geometry $P=S=16$. Studying only that point would show how the published configuration behaves, but not whether a blind spot is specific to it or follows the tokenisation parameters that generate it. The design therefore varies $P$ and $S$ to observe Chronos beyond the standard configuration. At fixed $P$, changing $S$ varies the overlap and the spacing of the stride family; at fixed $S$, changing $P$ varies the patch width and the spacing of the patch family. The $P=S$ runs provide contiguous, non-overlapping baselines at different temporal resolutions. Configurations with $S>P$ are excluded because their gaps discard input samples and would confound tokenisation geometry with data loss.

\subsection{Retraining}

Every geometry, including the $P=S=16$ baseline, is trained from scratch. Changing $P$ changes the dimension of the learned weights, while changing $S$ changes the patch sequence on which the inter-patch dynamics are learned. Only the official Chronos-Bolt-Tiny architectural configuration is loaded, after which $(P,S)$ is overridden before random initialisation and the data, optimiser, budget and seed are held fixed across runs. The complete construction, streaming-data and optimisation procedure is given in \autoref{sec:appRetraining}.

\subsubsection{Exploratory frequency sweep}
The exploratory sweep supplies pure sinusoids cross $[2,250]$\,Hz in one-hertz steps and at several phases. Their contexts are kept close to $512$ samples but shortened to a whole number of strides. For the behavioural reading, the median forecast is appended recursively until $512$ output samples have been generated, and the largest non-zero Fourier component gives the reconstructed frequency. For the representational reading, a linear probe acts on the output quantile head and distinguishes $f-1$\,Hz from $f+1$\,Hz under cross-validation that holds out phases. The former records what the model generates; the latter records whether the frequency remains linearly readable before that output is formed. The sweep framework is described in \autoref{sec:appSweep}.

A tone reconstructed at the wrong frequency but still recoverable points to a loss after the representation; failure in both readings is the stronger frequency hole. The sweep remains descriptive because it averages over phase and supplies neither a likelihood nor uncertainty. Its reconstruction and recovery views reproduce the diagnostic of Pagani et al.\autocite{paganiPreliminaryInsightsChronos2026}; the pure-tone views of \autoref{sec:appSweep} show frequency holes on both readings, geometry by geometry. Whether they persist once the tone sits on a TSMixup or KernelSynth background is the question the inferential stage answers, which is why that stage uses the generated signals rather than treating the noise-free case as sufficient evidence.

\subsubsection{Bayesian analysis}
Bayesian observations place a tone on a unit-variance TSMixup or KernelSynth background at signal-to-noise ratio $4$. Each candidate $f_k$ shares its background and phase with controls $f_k\pm\delta$, where $\delta$ is the largest offset not exceeding $0.25f_s/S$ that leaves both controls clear of both branches of $\mathcal F_{\mathrm{lock}}$; a site with no admissible pair is dropped. Phases are drawn from the non-redundant alignments available at each frequency, under the cap $S_f=\min\{f_s/\gcd(f,f_s)-1,\,N\}$ of Pagani et al.\autocite{paganiPreliminaryInsightsChronos2026}, which replaces independently drawn random phases by the distinct alignments a tone can actually take on the sample grid.

One exclusion applies to the paired observations, since it is a filter on the response. A triplet in which no arm is rebuilt at all, defined as $\max(R_k,R_-,R_+)\le 0.05$, is dropped: its contrast is a ratio of two noise floors, it carries no information about a difference between a candidate and its controls, and its logarithm is set by the stabilising constant of \autoref{eq:d2contrast} rather than by the data. The rule is evaluated on the triplet as a whole and never on the candidate arm alone, so it cannot remove candidates while keeping their controls, and it is applied before any model is fitted. A dropped triplet would enter with a contrast near zero, so retaining these rows would pull the population effect towards no effect. Excluding them therefore makes an effect easier to find, not harder, and any inconclusive behavioural result reported in \nameref{sec:seven} is not an artefact of the filter.

Model~A--C use tones carried by TSMixup and KernelSynth backgrounds; the location and movement tables used by D1 and D2 also retain the pure mode as the exact-degeneracy reference against which the two generated modes are read.

Model~A estimates the population recovery ratio at a candidate against its matched controls and the configuration-level variation used by $M1$. Its overlap-and-patch description is compared by leave-one-out cross-validation (LOO) with overlap only, patch size only and neither. Model~B estimates the multiplicative expansion of frequency-local prequential codelength and compares the stage-and-geometry-adjusted form with the literal unadjusted regression. Model~C estimates the spread of eight phase-bin offsets and compares the phase-dependent fit with its phase-free counterpart, separating the magnitude of a phase effect from whether including it predicts better.

Model~D1 asks which predicted grid locates the token-dispersion dips. For each signal mode, identical profiles are fitted under four labellings both grids, stride only, patch only and neither and ranked by LOO. Model~D2 then asks whether those sites move with the geometry: detections are assigned to the stride or patch branch, ambiguous sites are set aside, and each branch's fundamental is regressed on its predicted spacing. The median successive gap is fitted alongside as a robustness reading rather than substituted after inspection.

Every estimand is reported on its natural scale by its posterior median and $95\%$ credible interval, together with the probability of its preregistered threshold and, where defined, its region of practical equivalence. LOO is read through its uncertainty rather than by rank alone. Interpretation is then gated by convergence, posterior predictive checks pooled and stratified by configuration and generator, and the prior-scale and Gaussian-likelihood sensitivity fits. Only a posterior that passes those checks is carried into the three-way supported, refuted or inconclusive rule; numerical outcomes are reserved for \nameref{sec:seven}.
