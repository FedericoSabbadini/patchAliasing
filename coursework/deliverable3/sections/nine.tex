This report's techniques are built for an agent, not a reader, and the ontology of \autoref{sec:appendixA} makes that possible: it carries the tokenisation geometry as a first-class object, so every step below follows by rule from it. Four stages form one chain, each a different kind of statement.

Representation comes first. Each \pv{PatchedRepresentation} records its patch size, stride and overlap. Because a signal is evaluated under several geometries, a frequency-specific loss of observability attaches to the signal-configuration pair, not to Chronos-Bolt-Tiny alone: the same checkpoint can be safe for one plant and not another. From $(f_s,P,S)$ alone, \autoref{eq:locking-candidate} enumerates the candidate set by arithmetic, so a reasoner can list the sites of a geometry never measured.

Monitoring keeps it current. Stride- and patch-derived sites follow from $\mathcal{F}_{S}$ and $\mathcal{F}_{P}$ and are recomputed whenever the sampling interval or the geometry changes, a checkpoint change mattering only when it alters $(P,S)$. Placement matters more than cadence: monitoring must act on the raw telemetry before tokenisation, since a detector placed downstream inherits the blind spot it is meant to find. This is the one stage the forecaster cannot perform itself.

A geometric candidate does not by itself establish a loss, and explanation keeps the two apart. The Bayesian layer converts them into assessments: each \pv{ObservabilityAssessment} records the estimand, the effect size, the interval and the posterior probability behind its verdict. \autoref{eq:bayesian-evidence} separates the two directions of evidence, and because $\tau>1/2$ they cannot both hold; \valueof{PartiallyObservable} then denotes an inconclusive or an absent assessment. One line per assessment reaches a person: frequency, geometry, estimand with interval, state, and the control authority that follows. Its arithmetic half is derivable for any frequency; its measured half only where a fit exists, and a missing fit is reported, not defaulted away.

Action enters control through \autoref{eq:mode-selection}, not a policy of its own. A confirmed \valueof{StructurallyAliased} state selects \valueof{SafeMode} at the same priority as a sensor malfunction, under \autoref{eq:priority}: the battery holds and the grid balances the internal load while the array stays connected, the fault being in the model, not the plant. A merely \valueof{PartiallyObservable} state selects \valueof{ConservativeMode}, lowering the battery cap and the decision confidence without suspending dispatch. Both are bounded and reversible, degrading authority rather than substituting a correction the analysis does not support.

The chain assumes that assessment exists. Where no fit covers a candidate the state is \valueof{PartiallyObservable} by construction, so an unmeasured plant runs permanently at reduced authority: the cost is paid in capacity, not risk. The strategies of \autoref{sec:eight} change that; the third, redundancy across geometries, is the one this layer can already arbitrate: the switch it needs is the assessment the agent already computes.
