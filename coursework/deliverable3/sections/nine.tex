The techniques of this report are built to be used by an agent rather than read by a person, and the ontology of \autoref{sec:appendixA} is what makes that possible: it carries the tokenisation geometry as a first-class object, so everything below follows by rule from the knowledge base rather than by a decision taken outside it. Four stages form one chain, and each is a different kind of statement.

\subsection{Represent}

Each \pv{PatchedRepresentation} records its patch size, stride and derived overlap ratio. Since one signal may be evaluated under several geometries, a potential frequency-specific loss of observability attaches to the signal-configuration pair, not to Chronos-Bolt-Tiny alone. This is the distinction \nameref{sec:two} insists on and the reason the same checkpoint can be safe for one plant and not for another. From $(f_s,P,S)$ alone, \autoref{eq:locking-candidate} enumerates the candidate set by arithmetic: no experiment is needed to know \emph{where} to look, and a reasoner holding the configuration can list the sites of a geometry it has never seen measured.

\subsection{Monitor}

The declared geometry then fixes what to watch. Stride-derived sites are recomputed from $\mathcal{F}_{S}$ whenever the sampling interval or the stride changes, and patch-derived sites follow analytically from $\mathcal{F}_{P}$; a checkpoint change requires recomputation only when it changes the associated $(P,S)$. Placement matters more than frequency of checking: monitoring has to act on the raw telemetry \emph{before} tokenisation, because a detector placed downstream inherits the blind spot it is meant to identify. This is the one stage the forecaster cannot perform on its own, and it is also the cheapest, since the candidate set is closed-form and a spectral monitor evaluated only at those sites is a small fixed cost per cycle.

\subsection{Explain}

A geometric candidate does not by itself establish a loss. The Bayesian layer converts candidates into evidence-bearing assessments: each \pv{ObservabilityAssessment} records the estimand, the effect size, the credible interval and the posterior probability behind its verdict, so an operator can inspect why a frequency holds the status it does. \autoref{eq:bayesian-evidence} keeps the two directions of evidence apart, and because the threshold satisfies $\tau>1/2$ the positive and the negative reading cannot both hold for one assessment; \valueof{PartiallyObservable} then denotes an inconclusive \emph{or an absent} assessment rather than forcing uncertainty into confirmation or rejection.

What reaches a person is one line per assessment: the frequency, the geometry that makes it a candidate, the estimand with its interval, the resulting state, and the control authority that follows. Its arithmetic half is derivable for any frequency the plant presents; its measured half is filled only where a fit exists, and the absence of one is reported rather than defaulted away. That asymmetry is deliberate, and it is what keeps an explanation from claiming more than the evidence behind it.

\subsection{Act}

The assessment enters control through \autoref{eq:mode-selection} rather than through a policy of its own. A confirmed \valueof{StructurallyAliased} state selects \valueof{SafeMode} at the same priority as a sensor malfunction or an inverter trip, under the strict order of \autoref{eq:priority}: the battery holds and the grid balances the internal load, while the array stays connected, since the fault lies in what the model can represent and not in the plant. A merely \valueof{PartiallyObservable} state selects \valueof{ConservativeMode}, which lowers the battery cap and the decision confidence without suspending dispatch. Both responses are bounded and reversible, and both degrade authority rather than substituting a correction the analysis does not support: on this design the agent's contribution is not repairing a forecast but declining to propagate one it cannot vouch for.

\subsection{Where the agent's own limits lie}

The chain above assumes the assessment it acts on exists. Where no fit covers a candidate the state is \valueof{PartiallyObservable} by construction, so an unmeasured plant runs permanently at reduced authority, which is safe but not free: the cost of the design is paid in capacity whenever evidence is missing rather than in risk. The strategies of \nameref{sec:eight} are what change that, and the third of them, redundancy across geometries, is the one this layer is already equipped to arbitrate, since the switch it needs is the observability assessment the agent computes anyway.
