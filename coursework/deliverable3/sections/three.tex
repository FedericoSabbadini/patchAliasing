The ontology reproduced in \autoref{sec:appendixA} closes the loop from the signal to the agent decision. It represents the photovoltaic micro-grid, its telemetry and a single control agent, and it makes the tokenisation geometry a first-class object rather than a property of the model: a \pv{TimeSeriesSignal} has a \pv{PatchedRepresentation} carrying \pv{patchSize}, \pv{stride} and a derived \pv{overlapRatio}, and one signal may hold several, one per geometry $(P,S)$ under evaluation. Since the lock conditions are arithmetic on those same fields, the set of candidates is derivable from the knowledge base rather than hard-coded beside it, which is what lets an agent recompute it when a configuration or a sampling interval changes.

The point of contact with the Bayesian analysis is the following. An \pv{ObservabilityAssessment} does not derive a state from the geometry alone. It records the estimand, its effect size, its credible interval and its posterior probability, and assigns \valueof{StructurallyAliased} observability state only to a frequency that is a lock candidate and also carries confirmed evidence of loss.

Geometry alone yields a candidate, never a verdict: because the confirmation threshold exceeds one half, confirmed loss and confirmed preservation cannot both hold, and everything short of confirmed preservation is \valueof{PartiallyObservable}.

That distinction is what reaches the domain concepts, none of which is read off the raw telemetry. A \valueof{RampEvent}, or a \valueof{PartialCloud} generation context, is inferred from an observation whose short-horizon forecast the agent has already trusted, so a frequency the representation cannot hold is one whose signature cannot support that inference. The exposure is selective and the arithmetic says where: on one-minute telemetry with $P=16$ the patch comb falls at periods of $16/k$ minutes, the timescales of cloud-edge crossings and inverter duty cycling, while a broadband \valueof{InverterTrip} is untouched, since no comb hides a step. The predicted failure is therefore a missed distinction rather than a wrong number, two contexts differing only at a candidate frequency mapping towards one latent state and being misassigned silently. Hence the assessment, not the geometry, selects the mode: confirmed aliasing forces \valueof{SafeMode}, and \valueof{PartiallyObservable} forces \valueof{ConservativeMode}.

\subsection*{Control Agent}
This report's techniques are built for an agent and the ontology of \autoref{sec:appendixA} makes that possible: it carries the tokenisation geometry as a first-class object, so every step below follows by rule from it.

Each \pv{PatchedRepresentation} records its patch size, stride and overlap. Because a signal is evaluated under several geometries, a frequency-specific loss of observability attaches to the signal-configuration pair, not to Chronos-Bolt-Tiny alone: the same checkpoint can be safe for one photovoltaic plant and not another. From $(f_s,P,S)$ alone, \autoref{eq:locking-candidate} enumerates the candidate set by arithmetic, so a reasoner can list the sites of a geometry never measured.

Stride-derived and patch-derived sites follow from $\mathcal{F}_{S}$ and $\mathcal{F}_{P}$ and are recomputed whenever the sampling interval or the geometry changes, a checkpoint change mattering only when it alters $(P,S)$. Placement matters more than cadence: monitoring must act on the raw telemetry before tokenisation, since a detector placed downstream inherits the blind spot it is meant to find. This is the one stage the forecaster cannot perform itself.

A geometric candidate does not by itself establish a loss, and explanation keeps the two apart. The Bayesian layer converts them into assessments: each \pv{ObservabilityAssessment} records the estimand, the effect size, the interval and the posterior probability behind its verdict. \autoref{eq:bayesian-evidence} separates the two directions of evidence, and because $\tau>1/2$ they cannot both hold; \valueof{PartiallyObservable} then denotes an inconclusive or an absent assessment. One line per assessment reaches a person: frequency, geometry, estimand with interval, state, and the control authority that follows. Its arithmetic half is derivable for any frequency; its measured half only where a fit exists, and a missing fit is reported, not defaulted away.

Action enters control through \autoref{eq:mode-selection}, not a policy of its own. A confirmed \valueof{StructurallyAliased} state selects \valueof{SafeMode} at the same priority as a sensor malfunction, under \autoref{eq:priority}: the battery holds and the grid balances the internal load while the array stays connected, the fault being in the model, not the plant. A merely \valueof{PartiallyObservable} state selects \valueof{ConservativeMode}, lowering the battery cap and the decision confidence without suspending dispatch. Both are bounded and reversible, degrading authority rather than substituting a correction the analysis does not support.

Where no fit covers a candidate the state is \valueof{PartiallyObservable} by construction, so an unmeasured plant runs permanently at reduced authority: the cost is paid in capacity, not risk.
