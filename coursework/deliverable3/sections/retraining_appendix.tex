This appendix gives a high-level view of the training workflow, from selecting a patch geometry to saving its trained checkpoint. The same controlled procedure is applied to the fifteen geometries in \autoref{tab:hfModels}. At sweep level the geometry list is the only change: the geometry list is populated with the required $(P,S)$ pairs, crossed with seed $42$, and processed sequentially.

\subsection{Model construction}

The script obtains the architectural configuration of Chronos-Bolt-Tiny \autocite{AmazonChronosbolttinyHugging2025,FastAccurateZeroshot2024a}, but does not load its pretrained weights. It preserves the encoder and decoder dimensions, attention heads and layer counts, fixes the context and prediction lengths at $2048$ and $64$ samples, and retains the nine forecast quantiles from $0.1$ to $0.9$. Before instantiation it replaces \texttt{input\_patch\_size} by $P$ and \texttt{input\_patch\_stride} by $S$. The standard $P=S=16$ model is therefore retrained under the same budget rather than represented by the larger published checkpoint.

\subsection{Streaming training data}

The training data follow the Chronos pretraining mixture\autocite{ansariChronosLearningLanguage2024,autogluonChronosDatasets2024}: the $10$-million-series TSMixup corpus and the $1$-million-series KernelSynth corpus are loaded from \texttt{autogluon/chronos\_datasets} as streaming datasets, independently shuffled with seed $42$, and interleaved in a $9{:}1$ ratio. The shuffle buffer is $10{,}000$ examples rather than the published $100{,}000$ to avoid streaming timeouts.

For each series the iterator first requires at least $60+64$ samples, leaving a minimum of $60$ past positions before the $64$-sample forecast horizon, and rejects a series if more than $90\%$ of its values are missing. It then draws a missing-value rate uniformly between $0$ and $0.2$, replaces that fraction of observations with \texttt{NaN}, and samples one valid split point. The most recent $2048$ past values form the context; shorter histories are left-padded with \texttt{NaN}. Boolean masks accompany both arrays, and windows with an entirely missing context or target are discarded. This preserves missingness through the model's normalisation step while excluding unobserved target positions from the quantile loss.

Each geometry is trained for $100{,}000$ gradient updates with batches of $32$. The optimiser is AdamW, using its fused CUDA implementation where available, with learning rate $10^{-3}$, zero weight decay and gradient norm clipped at $1$. The learning rate decays linearly to zero with no warmup. Computation remains in \texttt{fp32}; TF32 matrix multiplication is enabled on compatible NVIDIA devices. These settings follow the published Chronos-T5 recipe, except for the common $100$k rather than $200$k step budget and the reduced shuffle buffer.
