Space saving (SV) for seven hierarchical frequency-band tasks at decoder blocks 0–3 and the final output of the retrained P=S=16 Chronos-Bolt-Tiny (training seed 42). The full design sweeps 2–250 Hz in 1 Hz steps at fs=512 Hz with ten distinct phases. All phases of a frequency stay in one of five class-stratified prequential blocks. The first block is encoded uniformly; every later block is predicted using only preceding blocks, with training-only standardisation, PCA (up to 20 components), and logistic regression. SV=1−L/n; negative values indicate expansion. The uniform first block limits attainable SV to 1−n_first/n (approximately 0.8). Parentheses give SV after label permutation with seed 42. The grouped-bar layout follows Pagani et al., Fig. 3; this is the repository's adapted five-block MDL protocol. https://arxiv.org/html/2605.06361v1
